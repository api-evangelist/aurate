Kinfield Founder and CEO Nichole Powell roots her company in an eternal reverence for expedition, wanderlust, and the pure joy of being outdoors. This new personal care brand pioneers non-toxic solutions for recreation, nature, and everyday use, launching with a trio of flagship products—Golden Hour (DEET-free insect repellent), Sunday Spray (cooling after-sun aloe mist), and Waterbalm (moisturizing balm for the face and body).
